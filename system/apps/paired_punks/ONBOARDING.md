# Pair up with PairedPunks

PairedPunks gives you and your hackathon partners (up to 10 people) one private space for your AI chats, prompts, replies and files, kept in sync through a private GitHub project.

You need an Imbue Studio workspace and a GitHub account.

## Everyone: get set up

1. **Add PairedPunks to your Studio.** In any Studio chat, send:
   `Add the PairedPunks template from https://github.com/chrisheuer/pairedpunks`
2. **Connect GitHub.** When Studio asks, approve GitHub access. PairedPunks uses it to create private projects and sync files.
3. **Find your PairedPunks name.** Open PairedPunks. Your name is on the start screen and at the top right, like `@mayaok`. It is your GitHub username.
4. **Send your name to your partner.** Click Copy, then send it by any chat, text or a sticky note.

## The primary member: start the project

One person starts the project. They are the primary member.

1. Open PairedPunks and click **Start a project**. Give it a name and the event.
2. In **Members**, paste each partner's PairedPunks name and click **Invite**. Repeat for each partner, up to 10 people in total.
3. Click the **upload button** at the top and share your first chat or file.

## Partners: join the project

1. Open PairedPunks and click the **project menu** at the top left.
2. Under **Join**, click the project your primary member invited you to.
3. Share a chat or file with the upload button. Your partner sees it on their next sync.

## Working together

- **Your chat** is the first tab. Each partner has their own tab, and **Side by side** shows your chat next to theirs.
- Hover over any prompt or reply to **copy** it, or **add it to your chat** and build on it.
- **Sync** (the circling arrows) pulls in your partners' latest. PairedPunks also syncs every 2 minutes while it is open.
- **Clone to my computer** (the download arrow) gives you the whole project on your own drive.

Chats can contain passwords or private details. Only share chats you are happy for every member to read.
